Real bread, baked by the harbour

We bake slow-fermented sourdough, viennoiserie and seasonal cakes every morning at our Wapping Wharf bakery, and supply wholesale bread to more than 30 cafes and restaurants across Bristol. Wholesale customers get weekly invoicing and a trade account.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 7am to 3pm.
